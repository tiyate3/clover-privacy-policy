Clover Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Clover helps you keep track of the little things that make each day special. This Privacy Policy explains how Clover handles your information and the choices available to you.

Support

For assistance with Clover, contact cloverproductteam@gmail.com.

1. Information Clover Stores

Clover stores information you enter into the app locally on your device. This may include:

  • Daily journal entries and their dates.
  • Lucky-day markers and entry timestamps.
  • App settings, reminder preferences and onboarding status.
  • Clover+ unlock status and review-prompt preferences.

Clover does not currently require an account or operate its own server to collect or store your journal entries. The app does not currently include its own analytics or advertising SDKs. Purchase checks are handled by a third-party service, as described in Section 7.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Clover can schedule reminders locally on your device. You can change or disable reminders in the app's Settings or through your device's notification settings.

Clover does not currently send remote push notifications.

3. Face ID and Touch ID

If you enable biometric app protection, Clover uses your device's authentication system to request verification. Clover does not receive or store your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data.

4. Widgets

Clover may offer Home Screen widgets that display selected information from your entries, such as a daily note or a reminder of a previous entry.

Widgets may access selected Clover data through the app's local storage or a shared storage area used by the app and its widget extension. This allows widgets to display relevant information without uploading your journal entries to Clover's own server.

Information displayed in a widget may be visible to others who can see your device's screen, depending on your device settings. Consider this when choosing what information to display.

Widget availability and displayed information may vary depending on your version of Clover and the settings available in the app.

5. Sharing Entries

Clover allows you to create and share a monthly recap image. You can choose which entries to include and whether to hide entry text.

When you share an image, the image and any included text are passed to the app or service you select through your device's sharing features. The selected service's own privacy policy governs how it handles that information.

6. Storage and Backups

Clover stores app data locally using your device's app storage. Depending on your device settings and backup configuration, some app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup.

Clover does not currently provide its own cloud-sync service. Apple and other backup providers handle information according to their own services, settings and privacy policies.

Deleting Clover or changing your backup settings may affect your locally stored entries. Existing backups may retain data until it is removed according to the applicable backup settings and policies.

7. Clover+ Purchases

Clover offers an optional one-time Clover+ purchase through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes transactions under its own terms and privacy practices. Clover does not receive your name, payment card details or billing information.

Clover uses RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management service, to check and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives purchase-related information and an anonymous app user ID that is not directly linked to your name or identity by Clover. RevenueCat does not receive your journal entries through this purchase integration.

For more information, see the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

You can restore a previous purchase using Restore Purchases in the app's Settings.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your entries remain in the app until you edit or delete them, clear the app's data, or remove the app, subject to your device's storage and backup behaviour.

Clover does not currently provide user accounts or operate a separate server that stores your journal entries. Backups may retain data according to your device and backup settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Clover is intended for a general audience. Clover does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Clover-operated server. The app does not currently require an account.

Parents and guardians should consider their device's privacy settings and any information shared through other apps or services.

10. International Users and Legal Rights

Clover is intended to be available internationally. Privacy rights and legal requirements vary by location. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, or other handling of personal information.

Because Clover stores journal entries locally and does not currently operate an account-based data service, many data-management actions can be performed directly within the app or through your device settings.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Clover's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy link provided in the app and on its App Store listing.
