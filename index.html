# Clover Privacy Policy **Last updated:** 23 October 2026 Clover helps you keep track of the little things that make each day special. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how Clover handles your information. ## Support Need help with Clover? Email us at **cloverproductteam@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. ## 1. Information Clover stores Clover stores information you enter into the app locally on your device. This may include: - Your written daily entries and their dates. - Your lucky-day markers and entry timestamps. - Your app settings, reminder preferences and onboarding status. - Your Clover+ unlock status and review-prompt preferences. Clover does not currently require an account. The app does not operate its own server to collect or store your journal entries, and it does not currently include its own analytics or advertising SDKs. Purchase checks are handled by a third-party service, described in section 7. ## 2. Notifications If you allow notifications, Clover can schedule reminders locally on your device. You can change or disable reminders in the app's Settings or through your device's notification settings. Clover does not currently send remote push notifications. ## 3. Face ID and Touch ID If you enable biometric app protection, Clover uses your device's authentication system to request verification. Clover does not receive or store your Face ID or Touch ID biometric data. ## 4. Widgets Clover may offer Home Screen widgets that display information from your entries, such as a daily note or a reminder of a previous entry. To display this information, widgets may access selected Clover data stored on your device through the app's local storage or a shared storage area used by the app and its widget extension. This access allows the widget to show relevant information without requiring Clover to upload your journal entries to its own server. Information displayed in a widget may be visible to anyone who can see your device's screen, depending on your device settings. Consider this when choosing what information to display. Widget availability and the information shown may depend on the version of Clover you use and the settings available in the app. ## 5. Sharing entries Clover lets you create and share a monthly recap image. You can choose which entries to include or hide entry text. When you share an image, the image and any included text are passed to the app or service you select through your device's sharing features. That service's own privacy policy applies to its handling of the information. ## 6. Storage and backups Clover stores app data locally using your device's app storage. Depending on your device settings and backup configuration, some app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup. Clover does not currently provide its own cloud-sync service. Clover cannot control how Apple or other backup providers handle data under their own services and settings. Deleting Clover or changing your device's backups may affect your locally stored entries. Data retained in existing backups may not be deleted immediately. ## 7. Clover+ purchases Clover offers an optional one-time Clover+ purchase through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes all transactions under its own terms and privacy practices. Clover does not receive your name, payment card details or billing information. To check and restore purchases, Clover uses RevenueCat, a third-party purchase management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user ID, which is not linked to your name or identity. RevenueCat does not receive your journal entries. You can read RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. You can restore a previous purchase at any time using Restore Purchases in the app's Settings. ## 8. Data retention and deletion Your entries remain in the app until you edit or delete them, clear the app's data, or remove the app, subject to device and backup behaviour. Clover does not currently have user accounts or a separate server containing journal entries to delete. Backups may retain data according to your device and backup settings. ## 9. Children's privacy Clover is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through a Clover-operated server. The app does not currently require an account. Parents and guardians should consider the device's privacy settings and any information shared through other apps or services. ## 10. International users and legal rights Clover is intended to be available internationally. Privacy rights and legal requirements vary by location. Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, or other handling of personal information. Because Clover stores journal entries locally and does not currently operate an account-based data service, many requests can be handled directly within the app or through your device settings. ## 11. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated when Clover's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy policy link provided in the app and on the app's store listing. ## 12.
